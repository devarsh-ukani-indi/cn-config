What it is

ClearNews is an independent news aggregator. It pulls stories from publishers' public RSS feeds and groups coverage of the same event, so you can see who is reporting what. Every story is attributed to its original publisher, with a link to read the full article at the source.

There is no account or sign-up to read, and your topic and source preferences stay on your device.

What we stand for

No engagement algorithm

Your chosen interests and sources decide your feed — not a system optimizing to keep you scrolling.

Original sources

Every article is credited to its publisher and links out to the original coverage.

Calm by default

A briefing to read, not a feed to escape. No ads, no outrage, no clutter.

Private by design

No account required. Your preferences stay on your device; we don't sell your data.
